Notes
- Art. 50 only covers buildings < 23 m. Buildings >= 23 m ("high-rise", อาคารสูง) fall under MR 33. NEED MR 33 text.
- "High-rise" = height >= 23 m; "extra-large" = total floor area >= 10,000 m2 (from MR 33, to be verified).

### MR 33 (1992), as amended through MR 42, 50, 64, 69. Source: ASA consolidated PDF (download.asa.or.th/03media/04law/cba/mr/mr35-33-upd69.pdf). Local: scratchpad/dl/mr33.txt

| Rule | Article | Content |
|---|---|---|
| High-rise | 1 | Occupiable building with height >= **23.00 m** (to roof slab; for pitched roofs to top of wall of top storey). |
| Extra-large | 1 | Residential/other use with floor area >= **10,000 m2** (total, or on any one floor), single building. |
| Site frontage | 2 | Total floor area <= 30,000 m2: one side of the site >= **12 m** long on a public road with right-of-way >= **10 m**, continuous to connect to another public road >= 10 m wide. If > 30,000 m2: >= 12 m frontage on a road >= **18 m**. The 12 m wide frontage strip must stay clear (fire-truck access) up to the building. |
| Fire access | 3 | Clear road, trafficable width >= **6.00 m**, all round the building. |
| Setback | 4 | Outermost edge of the building (above or below ground, excluding foundations) >= **6.00 m** from neighbour's boundary or public road. |
| Max FAR | 5 | Total floor area of ALL buildings on the site / site area <= **10 : 1**. |
| Open space | 6(1) | Residential: open space >= **30 percent of the site area**. (Commercial/other: 10 percent; any residential use means 30 percent.) |
| Definition | 1 | "Space" (ที่ว่าง) = area free of roof/structure; may include ponds, pools, waste areas, parking outside buildings, and structures <= 1.20 m high with nothing above. |

Interaction MR 55 vs MR 33 for a high-rise: MR 33 art. 4 (6 m from road and neighbours) is stricter than MR 55 art. 41 (2 m for roads > 20 m) and art. 50 (3 m). MR 55 art. 44 (2:1 height vs opposite road boundary) still applies.

### Bangkok Comprehensive Plan B.E. 2556 Ministerial Regulation, Royal Gazette vol 130 part 41ก, 16 May 2013.
Source: ASA copy of the Gazette PDF (download.asa.or.th/03media/04law/cpa/mr56-bma.pdf), 82 pages. Local text: scratchpad/dl/plan56.txt (Thai numerals).

Brown zone parameters (the closing clauses of each zone article):

| Zone | Article | FAR (max) | OSR = open space / total floor area (min) | Permeable planting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ย.5 | 12 | 4 : 1 | 7.5 % | |
| ย.6 | 13 | 4.5 : 1 | (not read) | |
| ย.7 | 14 | 5 : 1 | 6 % | >= 50 % of the open space |
| **ย.8** | 15 | **6 : 1** | **5 %** | >= 50 % of the open space |
| **ย.9** | 16 | **7 : 1** | **4.5 %** | >= 50 % of the open space |
| **ย.10** | 17 | **8 : 1** | **4 %** | >= 50 % of the open space |

- ย.8 to ย.10 are "very high density residential" (หนาแน่นมาก). ย.8 = inner city with scenery and natural environment protection. ย.9 = inner city within mass-transit service. ย.10 = inner city adjoining the central business district.
- Each clause also says: open space must not be below the minimum "space free of cover" under the building control law (MR 33/55 percentages above); and the FAR/OSR limits apply to ALL parcels split from the original plot, combined.
- Residential/condo is not on the prohibited-use lists. Prohibited-unless-exception items are hotels > 50 rooms, big commercial, offices, etc. (art. 15 (1) to (31)). Exceptions depend on road width (>= 12/16/30 m) or being within 500 m of a transit station platform (art. 37 measures from the platform boundary).
- Art. 36 (road-width and frontage tests for those excepted uses): road >= 10 m: plot with one side >= 12 m on that road, road continuous to another public road, one at >= 10 m and the other >= 8 m, plot within 200 m. Road >= 12 m: 12 m side, 12 m + 8 m, within 200 m. Road >= 16 m: 16 m side, 16 m + 12 m, within 300 m. Road >= 30 m: 30 m side, 30 m + 16 m, within 500 m. Applies to excepted non-residential uses, NOT plain residential.

FAR bonus (arts. 51 to 57). Total bonus from all measures is capped at **20 percent** of the FAR (art. 57).
| Measure | Article | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Below-market / low-income / existing-resident housing in project | 52 | up to 20 %. Added floor area <= 4 x the floor area given to that housing. Low income = below 80 % of the Bangkok average household income. Price >= 20 % below the project average per m2. Existing-resident housing within 5 km of their old community. |
| Water retention | 55 | 1 m3 per 50 m2 of site = 5 %. More storage scales proportionally, max 20 %. |
| Energy-conserving building (Thai Green Building Institute or equivalent certification) | 56 | level 1: 5 %, level 2: 10 %, level 3: 15 %, level 4: 20 %. |
| Public open space / park (public buildings only in ย.8-10, พ.2-5) | 53 | up to 20 %, added floor area <= 5 x the public open space. Applies to PUBLIC BUILDINGS (อาคารสาธารณะ). Open space that is only the mandatory building-law space does not count. |
| Public car parking near transit (public buildings) | 54 | up to 20 %, <= 30 m2 per extra car. |

Worked check: ย.10, plot of 800 m2: base FAR 8 -> 6,400 m2. With a 20 % bonus: 9.6 -> 7,680 m2. MR 33 art. 5 caps a high-rise at 10:1, so 9.6 is allowed. OSR 4 % of 7,680 = 307 m2, but MR 33 art. 6 needs 30 % of the SITE (240 m2 on 800 m2): compare the two, the larger binds. (OSR is per total floor area, MR 33 is per site area.)

Cross-check: a secondary site (Thai blog summary) gave ย.7 FAR 5 / OSR 6, ย.8 FAR 6 / OSR 5, ย.9 FAR 7 / OSR 4.5. Matches the Gazette. A user-supplied guess earlier (ย.7 = 4, ย.8 = 5, ย.9 = 6, ย.10 = 7) was off by one zone. Do not use it.

### CORRECTION: which codes are "Brown"
Plan art. 7(3) (Gazette text, line ~162 of plan56.txt): **ย.8 to ย.10 = brown (สีน้ำตาล) = high-density residential.** ย.5 to ย.7 are medium density (orange). ศ.1 / ศ.2 = light brown (conservation). Earlier I wrote "ย.7 to ย.10"; that was wrong. Brown = ย.8, ย.9, ย.10.
AI / search summaries got this wrong twice: one said ย.8-ย.10 are "medium density" and "high density = ย.11-ย.15". That is the DRAFT 4th-revision coding (ย.1-ย.15), not B.E. 2556. Primary text wins.

### MR 55 amendments after the yotathai copy: MR 66 (2559), MR 68 (2563). ASA consolidated "upd68" PDF. Local: scratchpad/dl/mr55b.txt

### Bangkok Building Control Ordinance B.E. 2544 (ข้อบัญญัติกรุงเทพมหานคร เรื่อง ควบคุมอาคาร). ASA PDF bb44-03.pdf. Local: scratchpad/dl/bb44.txt
This is the Bangkok-specific building ordinance. It sits alongside MR 33/55. Where both apply, the tool applies the stricter.
| Rule | Art. | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Height vs road | 49 | Same 2:1 rule vs the opposite side of the nearest public road. **Extra:** if the building is on, or within 100 m of, a public road >= 80 m wide, with an access to it >= 12 m wide, height is computed from the widest road. |
| Road setback | 50 | Same 3 m / 6 m / 1/10 / 2 m rules as MR 55 art. 41. Exempt: residential <= 3 storeys or <= 10 m and <= 1,000 m2. |
| Open space | 52(1) | Residential: open space >= **30 % of the LAND area** (MR 55 says 30 % of the largest floor; MR 33 says 30 % of the site for high-rise. Same result as MR 33 for a condo tower.) |
| Front open space | 52(6) | Buildings > 2 storeys or > 8 m (other than low residential): front open space >= 6 m wide; if > 3 storeys, >= **12 m wide**, continuous along >= **1/6 of the building's outer perimeter** (may include side space joined to the front), connected to an internal road >= 6 m wide out to the public road. Passing under the building needs >= 5 m clear height. |
| Large building (definition) | 1(101) | Total floor area > 2,000 m2, OR height >= 15 m and total floor area > 1,000 m2. |
| High-rise (definition) | 1(110) | >= 23 m, measured from ground to roof deck. |

### Parking (Bangkok). Ordinance B.E. 2544 arts. 83-88; MR 7 (B.E. 2517) as amended by MR 64 (2555)
| Rule | Source | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Who must provide | Ord. 83(3), MR 7 art. 2(3) | Condos / residential blocks where **each unit >= 60 m2**. Also every "large building" (Ord. 83(16), MR 7 art. 2(7)). |
| Condo rate | Ord. 84(3); MR 7 art. 3(1)(ค) | **1 space per unit** (in Bangkok). |
| Large building | Ord. 84(16); MR 7 art. 3(1)(ซ) | **1 space per 120 m2 of building floor area**, OR the sum of the per-use requirements, **whichever is greater**. |
| Rounding | Ord. 85 | Round up per use type. Compute per use and per building type; take the larger total. |
| Bay size | Ord. 86 | 90 deg: >= 2.40 x 5.00 m. Parallel or < 30 deg: 2.40 x 6.00 m. >= 30 deg: 2.40 x 5.50 m. Must connect directly to the drive aisle. (Academic source confirms the 2.40 x 5.00 change came from MR 41 (2537); MR 41 PDF text was not extractable, legacy font.) |
| Aisle | Ord. 88 | Entry/exit >= 6 m (one-way >= 3.50 m). Aisle: < 30 deg: 3.50 m; 30-60 deg: 5.50 m; > 60 deg: **6 m**. |
| Off-site parking | Ord. 87 | If on a separate, non-adjoining title: walking distance <= 200 m. |

Tool decision (interpretation, write it down): a condo tower is a "large building", so required spaces = max(units with area >= 60 m2 x 1 + other uses, ceil(total floor area / 120)). Units < 60 m2 do not trigger the per-unit rule, but the 1-per-120 m2 floor still applies. Parking area per space for massing: 90 deg bay (12 m2) + half of a 6 m aisle x 2.40 (7.2 m2) + ramps/circulation allowance -> use ~30 m2 gross per space (assumption, flag it). Whether parking floor area counts toward FAR: plan art. 54 exempts only EXTRA public parking. Normal parking counts as building floor area under MR 33's definition of floor area -> include in FAR (state as assumption; practice varies).

## CLOSED: regulatory + spatial inputs (2026-11-19)
Rule set the tool will implement for a condo in ย.8 / ย.9 / ย.10:
1. FAR max 6 / 7 / 8 (plan arts. 15-17), + bonus up to 20 % total (arts. 52, 55, 56, 57), hard cap 10:1 (MR 33 art. 5).
2. OSR min 5 / 4.5 / 4 % of total floor area; >= 50 % of it permeable planting (plan arts. 15-17).
3. Open space >= 30 % of site area (MR 33 art. 6, Ord. 52(1)). Binding = max(2, 3).
4. Height at every point <= 2 x distance to the opposite boundary of the road (MR 55 art. 44, Ord. 49); 80 m road exception (Ord. 49). Rooftop structures excluded (MR 68).
5. Road setback: < 6 m road: 3 m from centreline; < 10 m: 6 m from centreline; 10-20 m: road width / 10 from boundary; > 20 m: 2 m (MR 55 art. 41, Ord. 50).
6. High-rise (>= 23 m): 6 m from every boundary and the road (MR 33 art. 4); 6 m fire road around the building (MR 33 art. 3).
7. Below 23 m: 2 m (<= 9 m) / 3 m (9-23 m) from boundary for walls with openings (MR 55 art. 50).
8. High-rise / > 10,000 m2 site access: >= 12 m frontage on a road with >= 10 m ROW (>= 18 m ROW if > 30,000 m2) (MR 33 art. 2). Fail = only low-rise allowed.
9. Front open space >= 12 m wide along >= 1/6 of the building perimeter for buildings > 3 storeys (Ord. 52(6)).
10. Parking: max(1 per unit >= 60 m2, ceil(GFA / 120)); 2.40 x 5.00 m bays, 6 m aisles (Ord. 83-88; MR 7).
